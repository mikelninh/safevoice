## 🚨 HEADLINE CHANGE: BMJ "Gesetz gegen digitale Gewalt" (GgdG)

On **17 April 2026**, Federal Justice Minister Stefanie Hubig presented the draft **"Gesetz gegen digitale Gewalt"** (Law against Digital Violence). It is the most significant reform of digital-violence law in years and is **directly in SafeVoice's domain** (deepfakes, cyberstalking, intimate-image abuse) — yet **none of its new paragraphs currently exist in the SafeVoice schema.**

### New criminal offences introduced (NOT yet in SafeVoice schema)
| New § | Name (DE) | What it covers | Relevance to SafeVoice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **§ 184k StGB** (new) | Verletzung der Intimsphäre durch Bildaufnahmen | Unauthorised creation/spread of intimate imagery — incl. **pornographic Deepfakes, digital voyeurism, "Rache-Pornos"**, regardless of how produced (real or synthetic) | Overlaps & extends current **§ 201a StGB** (Intimate Image Violation). Cases SafeVoice classifies only under § 201a today should also surface § 184k. |
| **§ 201b StGB** (new) | Verletzung von Persönlichkeitsrechten durch täuschende Inhalte | Unauthorised dissemination of **non-sexual Deepfakes** capable of seriously harming reputation (e.g. faked footage of a person committing a crime). Satire excluded. | New class of harassment SafeVoice cannot currently map to any paragraph. |
| **§ 202e StGB** (new) | Unbefugte Überwachung mittels Informations- oder Kommunikationstechnik | **Cyberstalking via GPS trackers / spyware** and similar tech-enabled surveillance | Complements current **§ 238 StGB** (Nachstellung/Stalking). Tech-enabled stalking should also flag § 202e. |

### NetzDG / platform-enforcement impact (amends netzdg-3 schema entry)
- The draft imposes a **duty on social networks seated outside the EU to name an in-country Zustellungsbevollmächtigter** (domestic agent for service); for EU-seated providers a court may order this in individual cases.
- This strengthens the existing **NetzDG § 3 (netzdg-3)** platform-obligations entry SafeVoice already tracks and affects the "platform_submit" / complaint path in the court-prep output.

### Effective date
- **NOT yet in force.** Status as of 2026-08-12: Referentenentwurf (published 17 Apr 2026, stakeholder comment deadline 22 May 2026) → progressing through the parliamentary process (Bundestag). Transposition of the **EU Directive on combating violence against women (Directive (EU) 2024/1385)** is the underlying driver, with an EU deadline of **14 June 2027**.
- **Action:** Pre-emptively add the new paragraphs to the schema so SafeVoice is ready on enactment. No change to existing §§ 185/186/187/241/238/201a/303a-b penalties.
